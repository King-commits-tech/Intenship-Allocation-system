Allocation policy

Scoring formula

score = 100 × Σ(wᵢ × componentᵢ) / Σwᵢ

  • Skills: 0.75 × required coverage + 0.25 × preferred coverage
  • Projects: 0.5 × share of internship skills shown in projects + 0.5 × TF-IDF similarity
  • Interests: 0.5 × domain match + 0.4 × TF-IDF similarity + 0.1 × location fit
  • Academics: CGPA / 10 (neutral 0.5 if missing)
